# Cut changelog

## Unreleased

- Camera files with LPCM audio (Sony XAVC S and others) keep MP4 as the output format instead of switching to MOV.
- Smooth scrubbing on heavy video (4K, high bitrate, 10-bit 4:2:2): instant keyframe previews while dragging on the timeline.
- No keyframe lines on the timeline.
- No black flash at launch.
- Settings in their own window (Cut ▸ Settings…, ⌘,); the theme always follows the system.
- SF Symbols, 13 pt system text, one size for glyph buttons, pop-ups that are never cut off, small static toasts, a plain drop screen.

## 3.70.2 (first release)

LosslessCut 3.70.2, restyled for macOS: native title bar, system blue and greys, SF font, one outline icon set, macOS buttons and pop-ups, solid rounded dialogs, system colours for segments, its own name and icon. No update check, no LosslessCut news banner. Everything else works as in LosslessCut.
